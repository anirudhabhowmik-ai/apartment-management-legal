Delete Your Account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

If you wish to delete your Apartment Management account, you can do so using the steps below.

Option 1 — Delete from the app (recommended)

  • Open the Apartment Management app.
  • Go to the Profile tab (or the Set Up screen if you don't have a property yet).
  • Scroll to the bottom.
  • Tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the prompt.

Your account will be deleted immediately. You will be logged out and cannot log back in with the same number unless you choose to recover it.

Option 2 — Request by email

  • Send an email to anirudhabhowmikbuba123@gmail.com.
  • Subject: Delete my account
  • Include the mobile phone number you used to sign in.
  • We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login access — you cannot log in with the same number unless you choose to recover your account.
  • Any properties you own — along with all their members, staff, bills, expenses, and payment records.
  • Your access to all properties where you were a member, admin, or staff.
  • Your push notification tokens.

Recovering your account

If you deleted your account and want to come back, you can recover it by logging in again with the same phone number:

  • Enter your phone number on the login screen.
  • Verify the OTP.
  • When prompted, choose Recover Account.

Recovering gives you a fresh account — you will start with no properties and no memberships. Your old properties, members, and bills are not restored.

What is retained

To keep accurate property records for other users who shared an account with you, we retain the following:

  • Your name and phone number as part of historical property records (for example, tenant lists, payment history) that belong to the property owners you interacted with. This is necessary for the property owners' bookkeeping and legal compliance.
  • Payment records and audit logs may be retained for up to 7 years to meet tax and legal requirements.

These retained records are not linked to an active login and cannot be used to sign in to the app.
