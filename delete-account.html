Delete Your Account

Last updated: 15 October 2025

If you wish to delete your Apartment Management account and the personal data associated with it, you can do so using the steps below.

Option 1 — Delete from the app (recommended)

  • Open the Apartment Management app.
  • Go to the Profile tab.
  • Scroll to the bottom.
  • Tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the prompt. Your account will be scheduled for deletion.

If you do not see this option in your app version, please use Option 2.

Option 2 — Request by email

  • Send an email to anirudhabhowmikbuba123@gmail.com.
  • Subject: Delete my account
  • Include the mobile phone number you used to sign in.
  • We will confirm and process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, mobile phone number, and profile photo.
  • Your push notification token.
  • Any personal data associated with your login.

What may be retained

  • Records you created for an account you own (properties, staff, bills) may be retained if the account itself remains active with other members — but they will no longer be linked to your name.
  • Payment records and audit logs may be retained for up to 7 years to meet tax and legal requirements, but personal identifiers are anonymized.
